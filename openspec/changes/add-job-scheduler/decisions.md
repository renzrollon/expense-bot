| D5 | How is local time computed? | agent_resolved | `Intl.DateTimeFormat.formatToParts` with `hourCycle: "h23"`. The weekday is computed from the date with `Date.UTC`. The scheduler does not import the parser | docs/epic.md:533; src/parser/dates.ts:7-19 |
| D6 | What does "due" mean? | agent_resolved | A job's latest slot is the most recent local date and hour its schedule names, at or before the tick. The job is due when the tick is at most 3 hours after that slot | docs/epic.md:519-523 |
| D7 | What is the period in "one row per job per period"? | agent_resolved | The slot's local date, called the scheduled date. It is computed once per job per tick and read by every consumer: the record key, `/ping`, the job and the logs | docs/epic.md:141; docs/epic.md:522 |
| D8 | Is the 3-hour window inclusive? | agent_resolved | Yes. A run 180 minutes late runs, and one 181 or more minutes late is skipped. The epic says "later than the grace window" is skipped | docs/epic.md:523; docs/epic.md:530 |
| D9 | What is recorded as skipped? | agent_resolved | Only a scheduled date with no record, once the window has passed. A `failed` or `running` record keeps its status | docs/epic.md:523-524 |
| D10 | How is a run claimed atomically? | agent_resolved | `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE ... WHERE failed OR running past the lease ... RETURNING attempts`, as in `claimUpdate` | src/gateway/update-log.ts:44-62 |
| D11 | How long is the lease? | agent_resolved | 30 minutes. A cron invocation's wall time is capped at 15 minutes, and ticks are 60 minutes apart. Exactly 30 minutes is still held | Cloudflare Workers limits page, fetched 2026-09-30 ("Scheduled Workers have a maximum wall time of 15 minutes per invocation"); openspec/specs/bot-gateway/spec.md:255-257 |
| D12 | At most once or at least once? | agent_resolved | At least once per scheduled date. A killed attempt, or one whose outcome was not saved, is run again after the lease. Jobs must be safe to repeat | docs/epic.md:549 ("no second message is sent" is EB-10's duty); src/gateway/index.ts:130-135 |
| D13 | Is there a cap on attempts? | agent_resolved | No. The 3-hour window bounds the tries to 4 hourly ones | docs/epic.md:524 |
| D14 | In what order do due jobs run? | agent_resolved | One after another, in registration order, each isolated so that its failure does not stop the next | docs/epic.md:531; openspec/specs/bot-gateway/spec.md:391 |
| D15 | Where and how are schedules validated? | agent_resolved | In `createScheduler` at module scope, throwing the gateway's `RegistrationError`. Names match `^[a-z0-9_]{1,32}$`, hours run 0-23, weekdays 1-7, days 1-28, and all are whole numbers | openspec/changes/archive/2026-09-29-add-bot-gateway/design.md:473, 510; src/gateway/registry.ts:58-63, 74 |
| D16 | Which days of the month are allowed? | agent_resolved | 1 to 28. The only monthly job in the epic runs on the 1st, and no job asks for a clamping rule | docs/epic.md:43; docs/epic.md:541 |
| D17 | What does a tick do when the settings are invalid, or when no job is registered? | agent_resolved | Invalid settings: log `config_invalid`, run nothing, write nothing. No jobs: return before touching settings or the database | src/gateway/config.ts:25-33; docs/setup.md:100 |
| D18 | What happens when no chat id is stored? | agent_resolved | The chat id is read once per tick, only when a job will run. When none is stored, the job is not called and the run fails with `No allowed chat id is stored`, so it is retried within the window | archived gateway decision D42; src/gateway/settings.ts:3-11 |
| D19 | Which Telegram client do jobs get? | agent_resolved | grammY `Api` (grammY `1.46.0`, already pinned), built once per tick with the bot token and a `fetch` looked up at call time | src/gateway/bot.ts:17-22; src/gateway/registry.ts:43; package.json |
| D20 | Does `JobContext` change? | agent_resolved | It gains `scheduledDate: string`, and `now` is the clock at the job's start. The gateway design expected EB-09 to adjust this type | openspec/changes/archive/2026-09-29-add-bot-gateway/design.md:512, 808 |
| D21 | What is the `job_runs` schema? | agent_resolved | STRICT, with primary key `(job, scheduled_date)`, `scheduled_hour`, a `CHECK`-constrained status, `attempts`, `last_error`, and ISO UTC `created_at`, `started_at` and `finished_at`. A skipped record has 0 attempts and no start time | migrations/0001_bot_gateway.sql:1-13; docs/epic.md:141 |
| D22 | How does a tick read the records? | agent_resolved | One statement with `(job, scheduled_date) IN (VALUES ...)` for every job's slot | docs/epic.md:155 |
| D23 | What is logged, and what is kept of an error? | agent_resolved | One JSON line per outcome, with no error text. The error message is cut to 500 characters and stored in `last_error` | src/gateway/update-log.ts:28, 98; src/gateway/index.ts:198-201 |
| D24 | What does `/ping` show? | agent_resolved | `Jobs: none registered`, or one line per registered job: `not run yet`, or its state and slot, plus attempts for a failure | docs/epic.md:525; openspec/specs/bot-gateway/spec.md:529-531; src/core/index.ts:62-71 |
| D25 | How is a dead `running` record shown? | agent_resolved | As `interrupted` once its attempt started more than 30 minutes before `/ping` | D11 |
| D26 | Can a tick throw? | agent_resolved | No. Every failure is logged and the tick ends. Cloudflare does not retry cron invocations, and the next tick recovers from the records | src/gateway/index.ts:130-135 |
