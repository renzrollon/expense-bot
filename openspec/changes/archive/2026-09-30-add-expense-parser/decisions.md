| D18 | How is "the nearest such date" chosen? | agent_resolved | The candidates are that month and day in the current local year and in the year before. A candidate that is not a real date is dropped. The candidate with the smallest distance in days wins, a tie goes to the past, and the future check runs on the winner | docs/epic.md:278; docs/epic.md:296; docs/epic.md:298 |
| D19 | Are two date phrases that mean the same day a conflict? | agent_resolved | No. Only two different calendar dates are rejected. A phrase that is not a real date differs from every other phrase | docs/epic.md:279 |
| D20 | How are `-250` and `lunch - 250` read? | agent_resolved | A minus sign joined to the number makes the amount out of range. A hyphen with space around it is punctuation and is dropped | docs/epic.md:271; docs/epic.md:328 |
| D21 | Are numbers joined to letters or symbols amounts? | agent_resolved | No. Units (`5kg`), multipliers (`x2`), fractions (`1/2`), tags and links stay in the description. `k` is the only suffix that is read | docs/epic.md:275; docs/epic.md:305 |
| D22 | Is a number with more than 2 decimal places an amount? | agent_resolved | No, even when the extra digits are zeros, so `1.500` is never read as 1 peso 50. The digits are counted as written.
